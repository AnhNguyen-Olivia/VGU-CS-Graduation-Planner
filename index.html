    5. Key rules at a glance

    • Internship: 120 ECTS needed · ≥14 weeks, full-time (5 days × 8 h) · starts in SS2027 after Tet · grade = report 80% + presentation 20% · all documents in English.
    • IC (Internship Confirmation, signed and stamped) within the first 2 weeks. IR + IE (signed and stamped) have no deadline, but submit ASAP; the presentation is held only after IC, IE and IR (hard + soft copy) are in.
    • Thesis: 150 ECTS + proof of internship registration (IC) · 2 supervisors (1 from CSE, the other with ≥ Master’s degree) · approval ≤ 4 weeks · report due 9 weeks after approval · defense ≤ 1 month after submission.
    • Late thesis report = fail. The thesis with colloquium can only be repeated once. One extension (apply before the deadline, it moves only the report deadline). Title can change once, within the first 4 weeks.
    • Internship presentation must be done before the oral defense. Defense is onsite (at least one supervisor onsite).
    • Report formats: internship 20–35 pages, thesis 25–60 pages · 12 pt, single spacing, numbered pages · Turnitin similarity ≤ 15%. Thesis: 2 hard + 1 digital copy (may be submitted 1 week early).
    • Graduation: 180 ECTS + 4 German classes or A2 · assessments in April and October · one ceremony in November.
    • Status fee: thesis not submitted by 30 Nov 2027 (summer semester) or 31 May 2028 (winter semester) → fee for the next semester.
